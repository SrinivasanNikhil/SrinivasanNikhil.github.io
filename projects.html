Software I build for my own students and for curious kids: a platform for practicing SQL, a multiplayer game that quizzes you on databases, and hands-on science labs.

Course platform

Student Submission Tracker

A web app where students practice SQL and submit data-modeling work, and instructors manage sections, deadlines, and grading.

  • Queries run against real databases and are checked on rows, columns, and column names
  • AI feedback on SQL, and AI scoring of ER diagrams that an instructor reviews
  • Due dates, grace periods, and grade exports for each course section
Node.js · Express · MySQL · OpenAI

Learning game

Neon Snake

A real-time multiplayer 3D snake game with database quizzes. It's a companion to Richard T. Watson's open Data Management textbook.

  • A shared arena for each book chapter (3–10), with weekly top-ten leaderboards
  • A quiz appears after every ten orbs: right answers give a speed boost, wrong ones cost length
  • Questions adjust to each player's level, and every explanation links to the book section it covers
React · Three.js · Cloudflare Workers

Science for kids

Wonderlab

Interactive science labs where young explorers experiment with space, chemistry, ecosystems, and light.

  • Build a solar system by changing planet mass and orbits
  • Separate labs for chemistry, ecosystems, and light
  • A science notebook saved in the browser, with export and import between iPad and computer
